# Market research — Life Documentary (26 September 2026)

What exists, why it churns, what makes people come back, what AI costs, what earns trust, how the category is priced. Items marked **(unverified)** were not confirmed against a primary or second source. Sources at the end. `docs/VISION.md` draws its interaction rules from §3 and its pricing from §6.

## 1. Competitors

### Direct: lifelog to video
- **1 Second Everyday (1SE).** One 1–10 s clip per day, stitched into a "mash". Pro $9.99/mo or $49.99/yr; à-la-carte backup $4.49 and watermark removal $4.99. 4.8★ on 48K US ratings. Loved: longevity ("six years… to see my life day by day is so beautiful"), the year-end video. Complaints: features that used to be free moved behind the paywall, "hefty subscription", UI rewrites broke workflows, stability. Closest analogue; no narrative, no AI, no narration.
- **Google Photos Memories / Highlight Video / Ask Photos.** Free with 15 GB. Auto carousels, AI "Highlight Video" with music, Gemini "Ask Photos". Tested "My Week", a weekly filmstrip that Retro's founder said copied Retro (Jul 2024). Complaints: privacy of Gemini scanning intimate libraries; consumer content can be used to improve Google systems; a claimed default-on AI-training toggle (Jun 2026) **(unverified)**. Retention: install base plus "On this day".
- **Apple Photos Memories + Journal.** Memory Movies from a typed description, on device, iPhone 15 Pro+. Journal app free, on-device Journaling Suggestions API (photos, workouts, places, music; nothing leaves the device until tapped). No monetisation. Threat: Apple can ship a free private "movie of your memories" any cycle. The gap is narration, story and continuity across years.

### Adjacent: journaling
- **Day One.** Basic free; Silver $49.99/yr; Gold $74.99/yr (AI tier). E2E on all tiers, PDF/JSON export, book printing. Complaints: price, Android parity, "stagnant post-acquisition", weak book layouts. Made unlimited journals free (May 2025) against Apple Journal.
- **Journey.** $6.99/mo, $49.99/yr, $17.99 once, $199 lifetime. E2E sync, ePUB/DOCX/PDF export. Cross-platform is its wedge.

### Adjacent: memoir and legacy (gift market)
- **StoryWorth.** Weekly email question for a year, then a hardcover. $59 / $109 / $199 (Unlimited renews at $99). Overage $20/page. Complaints: the year starts at purchase, view-only family access, read-only after the year unless renewed. Almost entirely gift-driven.
- **Remento.** $99/yr or $12.99/mo. Weekly voice/video prompts; "Speech-to-Story" AI turns recordings into chapters; hardcover with QR codes linking to the original voice. 4.9★ Trustpilot. Complaints: AI rewrites "don't feel like they're in their loved ones' real voices"; QR codes depend on Remento's servers. **The strongest signal that users want the real voice preserved, not an AI paraphrase.**
- AI memoir apps (Autobiographer, Memoirji, StoriedLife, HereAfter, Storii…): crowded, voice in, prose out, none produce video.

### Adjacent: friend-first photo apps (grow, then churn)
- **BeReal.** 73.5M MAU (Aug 2022) → 23M (Jan 2024) → ~16M (Mar 2025); other trackers say 40M (figures conflict). Sold to Voodoo for €500M (Jun 2024). Why people left: "I like to use social media on my terms", gated feed, "boring real fast", late posts hollowed out the premise, Instagram copied it. Survivors engage hard (72% daily).
- **Lapse.** Invite-gated relaunch: 218K downloads in a day, #1 US, then downloads down 70% within weeks. $30M Series B (Feb 2024). Nov 2025: "costs of running the platform" forced removal of Feed, Profile and Chat; now a disposable-camera utility. Lesson: forced invites buy a spike, not a habit; media storage costs are real.
- **Locket.** Home-screen widget with friends' photos. 91M+ installs, 100K+ subscribers, profitable with 15 people. Gold $4/mo or $36/yr plus ads. Mechanics: a photo on unlock; weekly "Rollcall" Live Activity; "we're all doing this together".
- **Retro.** Weekly private photo journal for close friends; recaps; "Rewind" dial through the camera roll. ~1M users with **45.7% daily engagement** (TechCrunch, Dec 2025); $21M Series A at >$100M. ~$36/yr, no ads. Founder: "People take more photos than ever, but do less with them than ever." That sentence is Life Documentary's thesis.
- **Rewind / Limitless pendant.** Bought by Meta (Dec 2025); hardware stopped, service shut in EU/UK and others; refund demands. Always-on lifelogging is now owned by the least-trusted company: a positioning opening.
- **Snapchat Memories.** Free storage capped at 5 GB (Sep 2025), plans $1.99–$15.99/mo; called a "memory tax". People will pay to keep memories and resent being ransomed.
- **Year-in-review.** Spotify Wrapped 2025: 200M engaged users in 24 h, 500M shares on day one. TikTok Recap builds a personal video in the composer. Facebook "On This Day": 60M daily visitors, suppresses exes and the deceased.

## 2. Why photo and journal apps churn
- Day-30 retention median 4–7%. Photo & Video has the **lowest trial-to-paid conversion of any category, 22.2%** (RevenueCat 2026). ~72% of annual subscribers cancel in year 1; 35% in month 1.
- **AI apps churn faster.** RevenueCat 2026: AI apps convert 52% better and earn 41% higher year-1 LTV, but annual retention is 21.1% vs 30.7% and annual plans are cancelled 30% faster. The AI moment sells; only a habit keeps.
- Hard paywall vs freemium: Day-35 conversion 10.7% vs 2.1%. But 1SE and Snapchat show the cost of paywalling what was free.
- Forced growth loops fail (Lapse). Mandatory daily rituals fatigue (BeReal).
- Streaks work but are double-edged; journaling is emotionally heavier than a language lesson, and a missed day must not read as failure.
- Long-horizon memory products are judged on whether the company will exist in ten years (Day One, Limitless, Lapse; Remento's QR dependency; StoryWorth's lockout).
- Storage economics broke Lapse and forced Snapchat's cap.

## 3. What makes people come back
1. Tiny daily capture with a large delayed payoff (1SE year film; Retro weekly recap).
2. Lock and home-screen surfaces (Locket widget and Rollcall).
3. Close friends and family, not followers (Retro 45.7% daily, Locket "together").
4. Weekly cadence beats daily obligation; weekly tolerates missed days.
5. Nostalgia surfaces ("On This Day", Rewind), with painful memories filtered.
6. Prompts from context, on device (Apple Journaling Suggestions).
7. A yearly shareable recap as the acquisition event (Wrapped, TikTok Recap, 1SE year video).
8. Delayed reveal is a hook but insufficient alone (Lapse, Dispo).
9. Preserve the real voice (Remento's most-loved feature).
10. Gift-triggered onboarding of a parent (StoryWorth, Remento at ~$99).

## 4. AI capabilities and unit costs (2026)
- **Generative video** (per second, list prices Jul 2026): Veo 3.1 $0.75 standard / $0.15 fast / $0.05 lite; Kling 3.0 $0.10; Runway Gen-4.5 ~$0.12–0.15; Luma Ray 2 $0.04; Wan 2.6 $0.05. OpenAI Sora 2 API shutdown dated 24 Sep 2026: do not build on it. A 10 s clip costs $0.50–7.50. Conclusion: the user's footage is the content; generative video is at most later premium B-roll.
- **Narration** (per 1M characters): Google Cloud TTS $4 standard to $30 Chirp 3 HD; OpenAI $15–30; ElevenLabs $50 (Flash/Turbo) to $100 (v3, Multilingual v2). A 3-minute narration costs $0.01–0.27. Voice cloning needs explicit consent; ElevenLabs Professional Voice Clone is own-voice only.
- **Music**: ElevenLabs Music ≈ $0.15–0.20/min with commercial licence on paid plans; Stable Audio ~$0.20/track; Suno and Udio have no official API and active litigation. Licensed library music remains the safer default.
- **On-device understanding**: Apple Foundation Models (WWDC26) take image input, iPhone 15 Pro+; Google ML Kit labels free and local; CLIP/SigLIP-class embeddings run on device (Ente does face and semantic search locally).
- **LLM script**: a week's script (~20K in, 2K out) costs well under $0.10 on a mid-tier model.
- **Rough episode COGS**: LLM <$0.10 + TTS $0.03–0.30 + music $0.15–0.60 + optional generative B-roll $0.50–1.50 → **$0.30–2.50 per episode**. The high end exceeds a $50/yr subscription; generative video must be premium-only or absent.

## 5. Privacy and trust
- 90% worry about AI using their data without consent; 88% do not freely share personal information with AI tools (Malwarebytes, Mar 2026). 82% left a brand over data use in the past year (Relyance 2025).
- Backlash cases: Adobe ToS (Jun 2024) → rewritten in 18 days to say "no gen-AI training on customer content"; Meta EU training opt-in by default (2024–25); Google Gemini scanning Photos/Gmail (OECD.AI incident, Apr 2026); Snapchat memory tax; Limitless → Meta; 1SE paywalling backups.
- What earns trust: local-first processing (Apple Journal, Ente); E2E as a headline (Day One, Journey); open source and audits (Ente/Cure53); per-feature opt-in; a plain "we never train on your content"; full export in open formats; a survivability story.
- Design consequences for us: index on device, send only what an episode needs, never train, one-tap export of MP4 + JSON, a published shutdown promise.

## 6. Business model evidence
| Product | Model | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 1SE | Freemium sub + à-la-carte | $9.99/mo, $49.99/yr |
| Day One | Freemium, AI tier premium | $49.99 / $74.99 yr |
| Journey | Sub / once / lifetime | $6.99/mo, $49.99/yr, $199 lifetime |
| Retro | Freemium sub, no ads | ~$36/yr |
| Locket | Sub + ads | $4/mo or $36/yr; profitable |
| StoryWorth | Annual gift + book | $59 / $109 / $199 |
| Remento | Annual sub + book | $99/yr |
| Snapchat Memories | Storage tiers | $1.99–$15.99/mo |
| Google / Apple | Bundled | free |

Patterns: memory apps cluster at **$36–50/yr**; AI is a **+50% tier**; physical output reaches **$99–199** and unlocks gifting; family plans are rare (open space); storage is the hidden COGS, so tier it from day one.

**Gaps Life Documentary can own:** a narrated story across years (nobody does it); real voice preserved inside a film; partner and family co-authoring at $36–70/yr; a yearly premiere as the share event; local-first indexing with per-episode consent; an exportable artefact for legacy buyers.
