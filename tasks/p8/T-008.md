## T-008a · Storyline, cast and tagging use cases · needs T-007d

**Goal.** Use cases to create, rename, close, reopen and remove storylines and cast members, and to tag a moment, exist and are tested on the memory store; after any capture on Today, a Tag tray lets the person attach storylines and cast.

**Do.**
1. `src/application/storylines.ts`: `createStoryline(store, clock, ids, documentary, title)`, `renameStoryline(…, id, title)`, `closeStoryline(…, id)` (sets `closedAt = now`), `reopenStoryline(…, id)` (removes `closedAt`), `removeStoryline(…, id)` (soft delete, `deletedAt`). Titles are trimmed; empty or over 60 characters is a `words` error; a second **open** storyline with the same title (case-insensitive) is refused with a `words` error. Every change bumps `updatedAt`.
2. `src/application/cast.ts`: `addCastMember(…, name, relation?)`, `renameCastMember`, `setRelation(…, id, relation | null)`, `removeCastMember` (soft delete). Names trimmed, 1–40; relation trimmed, 1–40 or absent. Two people may share a name (the relation tells them apart).
3. `src/application/tagMoment.ts`: `tagMoment(store, clock, momentId, { storylineIds, castIds })` replaces the moment's tags (unique, at most 10 storylines and 20 cast, only non-deleted ids of the same documentary; anything else is a `words` error) and bumps `updatedAt`.
4. `src/features/today/TagTray.tsx`: after a capture is saved, a quiet "Tag" text button opens a `Tray` with two lists of chips (open storylines first, then cast), multi-select, and a single "Done" action; a "New storyline" and "Name a person" row inside the tray creates one inline. Reduced motion follows `Tray`.

**Tests.** Each use case on the memory store: create/rename/close/reopen/remove with `updatedAt` bumped; duplicate open title refused, duplicate of a closed one allowed; empty and 61-character titles refused; two cast members with the same name and different relations; `tagMoment` replaces tags, refuses a deleted storyline and an id from another documentary, refuses 11 storylines; a closed storyline is no longer offered by `todayQuestion` (through the real store and engine). `TagTray`: selecting two chips and Done tags the last moment; creating a storyline inline selects it.

**Acceptance.** Mobile typecheck, tests, lint, format, boundaries pass; no face, photo or voice field added anywhere.

## T-008b · Storylines and Cast screens · needs T-008a

**Goal.** Two calm screens list storylines and cast, and let the person manage them without leaving the film-like look.

**Do.**
1. `app/storylines.tsx` → `src/features/storylines/StorylinesScreen.tsx`: a `display34` heading, open storylines as quiet rows (title in `bodyStrong`, "since {month}" in `caption`, the count of tagged moments in `timecode`), then a "Closed" `label` and closed ones dimmed with `textSecondary`; one amber action "New storyline" opening a `Tray` with a `Field`; a row opens a `Tray` with rename, close / reopen and remove (remove asks once, in `words`, and explains the moments stay). Empty state: one plain `words` sentence about what a storyline is, with an example.

Empty state words explain that names stay on this phone and nobody's face or voice is recognised.
3. The Today screen's top row gains "Storylines", "Cast" (and "Settings" once T-008c lands) as `label` text links (accessibility role `link`).
4. The repositories supply a moment count per storyline (`countByStoryline` in `src/data/repositories/moments.ts`, owned by this task).
5. Design Lab sections "Storylines" and "Cast" render both screens with a seeded memory store; the Playwright spec visits them.

**Tests** (RNTL, memory store): lists render open then closed; creating through the tray adds a row; closing moves a row to "Closed"; removing asks, then hides the row; the empty states show their words; the amber action is the only `accent`-coloured element on each screen (check rendered styles).

**Acceptance.** Mobile typecheck, tests, lint, format, boundaries pass; CI `lab` job green with both screens in the video (artifact named under "UI visible"); DESIGN §6 checklist met; UNVERIFIED: device feel.

## T-008c · Settings and the local daily reminder · needs T-008b

**Goal.** A Settings screen sets one daily reminder ("Today's question") at a chosen time, on by default at 08:00 once the person allows notifications, scheduled locally so it works with no account and no network.

**Do.**
1. `npx expo install expo-notifications`; add its plugin to `app.json` (no extra strings needed on iOS for local notifications).
2. `src/domain/capturePorts.ts` gains `Reminders { permission(): Promise<PermissionState>; request(): Promise<PermissionState>; scheduleDaily(hour, minute, content): Promise<string>; cancel(id): Promise<void> }`; adapter `src/services/notifications/expoReminders.ts`; fake in `src/services/testing/fakeServices.ts`.
3. `src/application/reminders.ts`: `setDailyReminder(store, reminders, { enabled, hour, minute })` stores the setting in a new `settings` table (key/value; the next append-only migration after the last one on the base, with its migration test from a seeded earlier version, CLAUDE.md rule 6) and replaces the scheduled notification (cancel the old id, schedule the new one); `enabled: false` cancels. Content from `words`: title "Today's question", body "One question, ten seconds." — no streak, no guilt, no exclamation marks, never mentions missed days.
4. `app/settings.tsx` → `src/features/settings/SettingsScreen.tsx`: "Daily question" switch and time (hour and minute pickers in 15-minute steps; the chosen time in `timecode`); permission states: undetermined → the switch asks; denied → a `words` line and "Open Settings".
5. First launch after the first saved answer: if the permission is undetermined, a one-time quiet card on Today offers "Remind me each morning" (never before the first answer; dismissed forever with "Not now").

**Tests.** The new migration from a seeded database at the previous version keeps every row and adds `settings`; `setDailyReminder` schedules once with the right hour and minute, replaces on change, cancels when off, and survives a restart (reads the stored setting); the notification words pass the words test; the Settings screen toggles and changes the time; the denied state; the Today card appears only after the first answer and never again after "Not now".

**Acceptance.** Mobile typecheck, tests, lint, format, boundaries pass; Design Lab "Settings" section in the video; UNVERIFIED: the notification firing at the set time on a device, Android channel behaviour.
