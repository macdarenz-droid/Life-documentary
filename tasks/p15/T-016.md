# T-016 · P15 Render

Text for T-016a–e. Published 2026-09-27; this file does not change after publication. Fix lists are written in `tasks/TASKS.md` under the task.

## Preamble

**Goal of P15.** Turn a planned and narrated episode into a video. A pure timeline places every part of the episode (the cold open, the title, a slot for each bridge, the shots with their own sound, the closing and the tease), keeps the narrator off the person's voice, carries word timings for captions and tells the music when to duck. The render package draws that timeline in 9:16 and 16:9 with word-level captions. The server builds the render input from what is stored and starts the render through a port, with a fixture renderer for tests and Remotion Lambda for real renders. Starting renders from `EpisodePipeline` waits for P16, because the full clips a render needs are uploaded only when P16's "request clips" step asks the phone for them (D38: working copies are deleted after understanding).

**Read first:** `CLAUDE.md` (rules 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15), `docs/VISION.md` §5 and §8, `docs/design/DESIGN.md` §2, §4 and §8, `docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md` §4 "Render", §6, §7, §8, DECISIONS D9, D34, D37, D38, D39, D40, D41, `tasks/p14/T-015.md` (clips and word timings), `tasks/p13/T-014.md` (the plan, `shotFor`, `shotMs`), `packages/render/src/**` and `packages/story/src/render/**` (what P-A built).

**Decided here (DECISIONS D41).**
- **One owner for time.** `episodeTimeline` in `packages/story/src/render/` decides where everything sits and returns a `RenderManifestV2`; the render package only draws it and the server only gathers its input (ARCHITECTURE §6: the render owns how the episode looks, the story package owns what happens when). The plan's `lowerThirds[].atMs` was computed without bridge slots, so the timeline recomputes it and ignores the stored value.
- **The order.** Cold open, title card (3,000 ms), the scenes in plan order, the closing card (2,500 ms), then the tease. A scene whose bridge was kept opens with a bridge slot: 400 ms of lead-in, the clip, 600 ms of tail, with the scene's heading on screen. The narrator speaks only inside a bridge slot or the tease slot, so it never talks over the person, and the manifest check proves no narrator speech overlaps an answer. A scene without a kept bridge shows its heading as a small label over its first shot for 2,000 ms (or the shot's length if shorter) and adds no time.
- **Answers with their own sound, lightly cut.** An answer plays with its sound at full volume (video answers as video, voice answers over a card with the question they answered). When its words are exact (not approximate), its span is trimmed to 300 ms before the first word and 500 ms after the last, inside the plan's span ("lightly cut for pauses", VISION). Library clips keep their natural sound at a lower level. Photos are silent with Ken Burns.
- **Missing stays missing (rule 10).** The timeline takes only moments that have media. A shot whose moment is missing is dropped; a scene with no shots left is dropped with its bridge; a missing cold open, closing moment, cast name or storyline title means that part is left out, never replaced; a shot shorter than 500 ms after trimming is dropped; a question longer than its field allows is left out, never cut; with no shots at all there is nothing to render (`null`). The server rebuilds the brief when it renders, so a moment deleted or made `localOnly` since planning is never rendered (rule 8).
- **Speech drives captions and music.** Every answer and every narration clip becomes a speech unit on the manifest, with its words in episode time. Captions are drawn from speech units (word-level pages via `@remotion/captions`, DESIGN §4), and the music ducks under every speech unit, not only the narrator. Words marked approximate get pages without the current-word highlight.
- **Mixing.** Answers and narration at volume 1, clips at 0.4, music at gain 0.6 ducking to 0.15 with 300 ms ramps, a 1 s fade-in and a 1.5 s fade-out. Remotion does not normalise loudness (its mix is a plain sum), so levels are fixed constants and listening to a real episode is a device-check item.
- **Media tags.** Video stays on `OffthreadVideo`: iPhones record HEVC by default, and `@remotion/media`'s `Video` cannot decode H.265 during rendering (it falls back to `OffthreadVideo` anyway) and needs CORS on every source. Audio uses `Html5Audio`, since `Audio` from `remotion` is its deprecated alias from 4.0.358.
- **Two shapes.** 9:16 (1080×1920) is the episode; 16:9 (1920×1080) is an export rendered on request, never by default. Portrait fills the frame (cover); landscape shows portrait footage whole on the background colour (pillarbox), without a blurred copy. The cold open is letterboxed: a true 2.39:1 band in 16:9. In 9:16 a 2.39:1 band would cut a talking person to a strip, so portrait uses bars of 10% of the height at top and bottom.
- **Captions are burned in.** The bundled FFmpeg has no subtitle encoders, so no subtitle track is embedded; burned-in captions meet WCAG's captions requirement. A sidecar file for the in-app player is later work (P16).
- **Rendering on Lambda from a Worker.** Remotion's Lambda client does not run on Cloudflare Workers (its docs say so, and it imports Node modules), so the Worker calls AWS Lambda's `Invoke` API directly, signed with `aws4fetch`. It sends the same `start` and `status` payloads that Remotion's official Python client (`remotion-lambda` 4.0.529, `remotionclient.py` and `models.py`) sends with a plain synchronous invoke, and parses the response the same way (concatenated JSON objects, the last one counts). The payload must match the deployed function's version exactly, so a Node test compares ours with `LambdaClientInternals.makeLambdaRenderMediaPayload` from `@remotion/lambda-client` 4.0.529.
- **Polling, not webhooks.** The render step asks for progress (the `status` routine) instead of receiving Remotion's webhook. That means no public endpoint and no signature secret, and Remotion's webhook has no replay protection.
- **Where media comes from and goes.** Lambda reads each file through a presigned R2 GET URL that lives 15 minutes (ARCHITECTURE §7) and is made only for keys under the documentary owner's `u/{userId}/{documentaryId}/` prefix. Lambda writes the finished MP4 straight to R2 through Remotion's `outName` with an `s3OutputProvider` (R2's S3 endpoint). Both need an R2 API token scoped to the media bucket. This revisits D34, which avoided R2 S3 credentials for uploads: renders run outside the Worker, so they cannot use the binding. Remotion strips these keys from its stored render metadata. Its working files in its own S3 bucket are deleted after one day (`deleteAfter: '1-day'`), and the output uses `privacy: 'no-acl'`.
- **Keys and versions.** The episode's MP4 is `u/{userId}/{documentaryId}/episodes/{episodeId}/v{renderVersion}.mp4`, and the 16:9 export is `…/v{renderVersion}-wide.mp4`. They live under `u/`, so the `tmp/` rule never deletes them and account deletion finds them by prefix. This replaces the stale `users/{uid}/episodes/…` line in ARCHITECTURE. A finished 9:16 render sets the episode's `renderVersion`, `mp4Key` and `durationMs`. The move to `ready` and the push are P16's.
- **Cost.** One ledger row per episode for step `render`, provider `remotionLambda`, unit `render` (units 1), in µUSD from the progress's accrued cost estimate, rounded up. The Remotion licence is free while the company has at most 3 people (D9); a per-render licence cost is added when the owner says the team has grown.

**Stack (checked 2026-09-27).**
- Remotion 4.0.529 (all Remotion packages pinned exactly, D25). `@remotion/captions`: `Caption { text, startMs, endMs, timestampMs: number | null, confidence: number | null }`; `createTikTokStyleCaptions({ captions, combineTokensWithinMilliseconds, breakOnSilenceAfterMilliseconds? }) → { pages: { text, startMs, durationMs, tokens: { text, fromMs, toMs }[] }[] }`. A new page starts only at a caption whose `text` begins with a space, so every word after the first carries its leading space. A page's `durationMs` runs until the next page starts, so it covers silences, and the caller caps it. The container needs `white-space: pre`. The package is MIT with no dependencies and runs in Node and in a composition.
- `OffthreadVideo`: `muted`, `volume` (number or `(frame) => number`, frame 0 when the media starts, `trimBefore` does not shift it), `trimBefore`/`trimAfter` in frames; no `loop` (use `<Loop>`). `Html5Audio` has the same volume semantics. A volume of 100 or more throws. The renderer rounds a volume curve to steps of about 0.01.
- `calculateMetadata({ props }) → { durationInFrames, width, height, fps }`, which runs once per render.
- Lambda (`@remotion/lambda` 4.0.529, for the deploy script only):
  - `deploySite({ entryPoint, bucketName, region, siteName })` → `{ serveUrl }`.
  - `deployFunction({ region, timeoutInSeconds 15–900, memorySizeInMb 512–10240, diskSizeInMb (default 2048), createCloudWatchLogGroup, cloudWatchLogRetentionPeriodInDays })` → `{ functionName }`. The runtime is arm64.
  - Start and status go through `POST https://lambda.{region}.amazonaws.com/2015-03-31/functions/{name}/invocations` (synchronous). Payloads over about 200 KB would need S3; ours are far smaller and are refused above 200 KB.
  - Progress gives `done`, `fatalErrorEncountered`, `errors`, `overallProgress`, `costs.accruedSoFar` (USD) and `outputSizeInBytes`. The output's `key` must match `/^[0-9a-zA-Z-!_.*'()/]+$/`.
- `aws4fetch` 1.0.20 (MIT; SigV4 in Workers; `AwsClient.sign(url, { aws: { signQuery: true } })` for presigned URLs), imported only under `apps/api/src/providers/**`.
- Open issue remotion#11704 reports audio about 43 ms late in rendered MP4s. It is not fixed in 4.0.529 and is noted for the device check.

## T-016a · The episode timeline · needs T-014a, T-015a

**Goal.** A pure function places every part of an episode on one timeline and returns a checked `RenderManifestV2`.

**Do.**
1. `packages/contracts/src/render/renderManifest.ts` gains `RenderManifestV2` (keep `KenBurns`; `RenderManifestV1` stays until T-016b):
   - `version: 2`; `format: { width: 1080, height: 1920 } | { width: 1920, height: 1080 }`; `fps: 30`; `durationMs` 1..360000.
   - `segments` 1..40. Each segment is `{ fromMs, toMs }` plus one of:
     - `coldOpen { media }`
     - `title { label 1..40, text 1..60, subtitle? 1..80 }`
     - `sceneOpen { heading 1..60, background? }`
     - `shot { media }`
     - `closing { text 1..80, credit: 'AI-narrated', background? }`
     - `tease { label 1..40, storyline? 1..60 }`
   - `media` is one of:
     - `{ type: 'video', src, inMs ≥ 0, sound: 'full' | 'natural' | 'none' }`
     - `{ type: 'voice', src, inMs ≥ 0, question? 1..200 }`
     - `{ type: 'photo', src, kenBurns? }`
   - `background` is one of:
     - `{ type: 'photo', src, kenBurns? }`
     - `{ type: 'video', src, inMs }`, which is muted.
   - `narration` 0..6 × `{ src, atMs, durationMs ≥ 200 }`.
   - `speech` 0..40 × `{ kind: 'person' | 'narrator', fromMs, toMs, captions: boolean, approximate: boolean, words 0..200 × { text 1..60, fromMs, toMs } }`.
   - `sceneLabels` 0..5 × `{ atMs, durationMs, text 1..60 }`.
   - `lowerThirds` 0..20 × `{ atMs, durationMs 1000..6000, name 1..40, relation? 1..40 }`.
   - `music?: { src, gain 0..1, duckTo 0..1 }`.
   - Every `toMs` is greater than its `fromMs`.
2. `packages/story/src/render/episodeTimeline.ts`: `episodeTimeline(input) → RenderManifestV2 | null`.
   - The input is:
     - `plan` (`EpisodePlanV1`) and `format: 'portrait' | 'landscape'`.
     - `moments`: `{ momentId, kind: 'answer' | 'clip' | 'photo', media: { type: 'video' | 'voice' | 'photo', src, durationMs? }, question?, words?: { words: TimedWord[], approximate } }[]`. These are only the moments that have media.
     - `cast: { id, name, relation? }[]`, `storylines: { id, title }[]`, `episodeNumber`, `weekStart`, `weekEnd`.
     - `narration`: the kept clips `{ kind: 'bridge' | 'tease', sceneIndex?, src, durationMs, words: TimedWord[] }[]`.
     - `music?: { src }`.
   - Build in this order:
     - **Cold open.** Use the plan's span, then trim (below) when the moment is an answer. Media is `sound: 'full'` for a video answer and `'natural'` for a clip; a photo gets `shotMs`.
     - **Title.** 3,000 ms, with `label` = `episodeWords.label(n)` ("Episode 12"), `text` = the plan's title, and `subtitle` = the plan's subtitle or `episodeWords.dates(weekStart, weekEnd)`.
     - **Each scene.** First the bridge slot if its clip was kept: 400 + clip + 600 ms, `background` = the scene's first photo, else its first clip, else none. Then its shots:
       - an answer's span is the shot's span or `[0, durationMs]`, trimmed;
       - a clip's span is `inMs`..`outMs` from the shot, or `shotMs`;
       - a photo lasts `shotMs`, with the shot's `kenBurns` or 1→1.06.
     - **Closing.** 2,500 ms, `text` = `episodeWords.closing`, with the closing moment's photo, clip or video answer (muted) as background.
     - **Tease.** If its clip was kept: 400 + clip + 600 ms, `label` = `episodeWords.next`, `storyline` = the tease storyline's title.
   - **Trim** (answers with exact words only): the span becomes [max(start, first word − 300), min(end, last word + 500)], using the words inside the span.
   - **Speech.**
     - Each answer segment (cold open or shot) gets a `person` unit over the segment, with the words inside its span shifted to episode time and clamped to the segment. `captions` is the scene's `captionsFromTranscript`, and true for the cold open.
     - Each narration clip gets a `narrator` unit over the clip, with `captions: true`. A narration entry is placed at its slot's start + 400.
   - **Scene labels.** A scene without a kept bridge gets a label at its first shot's start.
   - **Lower thirds.** Each plan lower third starts at its moment's first scene shot + 500 ms and lasts min(3,000, that shot − 500) ms. It is dropped when that is under 1,000 ms, when the cast id is unknown, or when it would overlap an earlier one.
   - **Music.** Gain 0.6 and `duckTo` 0.15, only when `music` is given.
   - **Output.** Parse with the contract and return `null` when no shot is left.
3. `manifestErrors(m) → string[]`:
   - the segments are contiguous from 0 to `durationMs`, and each is at least 500 ms;
   - there is exactly one title; a cold open, if any, is first; the closing comes after every shot; a tease, if any, is last;
   - every narration entry lies inside a `sceneOpen` or `tease` segment;
   - no two speech units overlap (so no narrator unit overlaps a person unit);
   - every word lies inside its unit;
   - lower thirds and scene labels end within `durationMs` and lower thirds do not overlap.

   `episodeTimeline` throws when its own result has errors.
4. `musicVolumeAt(ms, m)` for v2: gain, ducked to `duckTo` under every speech unit with 300 ms ramps, times a fade-in over the first 1,000 ms and a fade-out over the last 1,500 ms; 0 without music. `manifestDurationMs(m)` = `m.durationMs`.
5. `packages/story/src/words/episode.ts`: `episodeWords` = `{ label(n) → "Episode n", dates(weekStart, weekEnd) → "12 to 18 October" (months differ: "29 September to 5 October"), closing: "See you next week.", next: "Next week" }`, covered by the words test.

**Tests.**
- A plan with a cold open, three scenes (bridges kept for scenes 1 and 3), a closing, a tease and two lower thirds gives segments in the right order and lengths, with narration inside the slots. `manifestErrors` is empty.
- An answer with exact words is trimmed to 300 ms before and 500 ms after its words; approximate words leave it untrimmed.
- Dropped parts:
  - a shot whose moment is missing is dropped;
  - a scene with no shots left is dropped with its bridge;
  - a missing cold-open moment gives no cold open;
  - a tease whose storyline is unknown has no storyline line;
  - no media at all gives `null`.
- A lower third lands 500 ms into its moment's first shot after a bridge slot. An unknown cast id is dropped.
- `manifestErrors` flags a hand-made manifest where narration overlaps a person unit, and one with a gap between segments.
- `musicVolumeAt` is at `duckTo` in the middle of an answer and of a bridge, at gain between speech, and 0 at 0 ms and at the end.
- The 16:9 format carries the 1920×1080 size.
- `episodeWords.dates` works within a month and across two.
- The contract refuses a word that ends before it starts and 41 segments.

**Acceptance.** Contracts and story typecheck, tests, lint, format, boundaries and the words test pass; `packages/story` still imports only `@life/contracts` and `zod`.

## T-016b · The episode, drawn · needs T-016a

**Goal.** The render package draws a v2 manifest in both shapes with every part's sound where it belongs, and CI proves it on fixtures.

**Do.**
1. `Root.tsx`: the `Episode` composition takes `RenderManifestV2` (schema) and its `calculateMetadata` returns the manifest's width, height, 30 fps and `msToFrames(durationMs)`.
2. `Episode.tsx` draws each segment in a `Sequence`:
   - `ColdOpen`: the media with its sound and the letterbox (bars of 10% of the height in 9:16; a 2.39:1 band in 16:9).
   - `TitleCard`: the existing title card with `label`, `text` and `subtitle`.
   - `SceneOpen`: the heading as a small tracked label, DESIGN §4, over the background, dimmed, or over the background colour.
   - `Shot`:
     - a video plays with `sound` `full` (volume 1), `natural` (0.4) or `none` (muted), starting at `inMs`;
     - a voice answer plays its audio (`Html5Audio`) under a card showing `question` in the display face;
     - a photo gets Ken Burns.
   - `Closing`: text and credit over the dimmed background.
   - `Tease`: label and storyline.

   Then the scene labels, lower thirds, grain, narration (`Html5Audio` in `Sequence`s) and music (`Html5Audio`, volume from `musicVolumeAt`). Cover in 9:16 and contain on the background colour in 16:9, from `useVideoConfig()`. Reduced motion keeps Ken Burns and reveals still, as today.
3. Remove `RenderManifestV1`, its v1 story helpers (`shotStartsMs`, the v1 `validateTimeline`, the v1 `musicVolumeAt`) and their tests; nothing stored uses v1. `fixtureManifest(format)` now returns `episodeTimeline` over fixture input:
   - a cold open (a video answer);
   - a scene with a kept bridge, a video answer and a photo;
   - a scene without a bridge, with a clip and a voice answer;
   - a lower third, the closing, a kept tease and music.
4. Fixtures:
   - `make-fixtures.mts` also renders `answer.mp4` (the fixture clip's picture with `voice.wav`'s sound, 4 s) and writes `answer-voice.wav` (the voice tone, 4 s).
   - `render-fixture.mts` renders both formats and checks each:
     - the size;
     - the duration within ±200 ms of `durationMs`;
     - an audio stream is present.
   - On the 9:16 file it also measures the integrated loudness (FFmpeg's `loudnorm` with `print_format=json`, `input_i`; the bundled build has no `volumedetect`) of the cold-open window and of the closing window. It checks the cold open is at least 6 LU louder: the answer's own sound is present, where the closing has only music.
   - Confirm once, and say so in the report, that muting answers makes this check fail.
   - CI's `render` job uploads both files.

**Tests.** The render package's tests for any pure helper added (letterbox sizes for both shapes; media fit); story tests for the v2 `fixtureManifest` (`manifestErrors` empty in both shapes); the CI render job as above.

**Allowed test changes.** The v1 timeline tests in `packages/story` and the v1 contract tests are removed with the code they test. Each behaviour they covered (durations, overlaps, music ducking) has a v2 test in T-016a.

**Acceptance.** Typecheck, lint, format, boundaries, tests, build and the CI render job pass. Frame checks by eye in the report: one frame each of the cold open, a bridge slot, a voice answer, a lower third and the closing in both shapes (as images attached to the PR comment, not committed).

## T-016c · Word-level captions · needs T-016b

**Goal.** Captions follow speech word by word, as DESIGN §4 asks, in both shapes.

**Do.**
1. `packages/render/package.json` adds `@remotion/captions` 4.0.529 (exact).
2. `packages/render/src/episode/captionPages.ts` has `captionPages(unit) → { fromMs, toMs, words: { text, fromMs, toMs }[] }[]`.
   - The unit's words become `Caption`s, with a leading space on every word after the first.
   - They are paged with `createTikTokStyleCaptions` (`combineTokensWithinMilliseconds: 1200`, `breakOnSilenceAfterMilliseconds: 800`).
   - Each page ends at the earliest of: the next page's start, its last word's end + 400 ms, and the unit's end.
   - A unit with `captions: false` or no words gives no pages.
3. `Captions.tsx` draws the page under the current time:
   - Inter 600 on the scrim (the existing `captionScrim`), at most two lines, `white-space: pre`.
   - The current word is in full text colour and the rest in the muted text colour, unless the unit is approximate; then the whole page is in full colour.
   - Placement is above the bottom safe area in 9:16 and in the lower band in 16:9.
   - No caption during the title, closing and tease cards except the tease's own narrator words.

**Tests.**
- `captionPages`:
  - a ten-word answer makes pages no longer than 1.2 s of speech, and a silence of 1 s starts a new page;
  - the last page ends 400 ms after its last word;
  - words without spaces never merge into one page;
  - `captions: false` gives none.
- A still of a caption frame in both shapes appears in the report.

**Acceptance.** Render typecheck, tests, lint, format, boundaries and the CI render job pass. The `@remotion/captions` licence is MIT.

## T-016d · The render input and the renderer port · needs T-014c, T-015c, T-016a

**Goal.** The server can build a checked render input for an episode from what is stored, start a render through a port, follow it to the end, and record the file and its cost, all tested with a fixture renderer.

**Do.**
1. **The port.** `Renderer` in `ports.ts`:
   - `start({ manifest, composition: 'Episode', outKey }) → { renderId, bucketName }`;
   - `progress({ renderId, bucketName }) → { state: 'rendering', fraction } | { state: 'done', costUsd?, bytes? } | { state: 'failed', reason }`, where `reason` is a short text with no user content.

   `providers/fixture/renderer.ts` stores the request and, on `start`, puts a small MP4 (a byte constant in its own file) at `outKey` in R2. `progress` reports `done` after a number of calls a test can set (default 1), or what a test sets (`failed`). It is reset with the other fixtures. `pipelineProviders` returns it only with `PROVIDERS: 'fixture'`, and the real one arrives in T-016e.
2. **The presigner.** `providers/r2/presign.ts`: `presignGet(env, key) → URL string`, valid for 900 s, using `aws4fetch` against `https://{R2_ACCOUNT_ID}.r2.cloudflarestorage.com/{R2_BUCKET}/{key}`.
   - `Env` gains `R2_ACCOUNT_ID?`, `R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID?` and `R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY?` (secrets) and `R2_BUCKET` (a var). Without them it throws `Rendering is not set up`.
   - Add `aws4fetch` to the boundary rule's vendor list.
3. **Data.** The next D1 migration adds `renders`, with its migration test:
   - columns: id, episode, plan version, render version, format `portrait` | `landscape`, vendor render id, bucket, out key, state `rendering` | `done` | `failed`, reason, started at, finished at;
   - `episode_id` references `episodes` with ON DELETE CASCADE.

   `data/repositories/renders.ts`: `put`, `get`, `setState`. `episodes.ts` gains `setRender(episodeId, renderVersion, mp4Key, durationMs)`. `mediaKeys.ts` gains `episodeKey(userId, documentaryId, episodeId, renderVersion, format)`, where the user is the documentary's `owner_user_id`. `CostLedgerRow` gains step `render`, provider `remotionLambda`, unit `render`, and `renderMicroUsd(costUsd)` = ⌈costUsd × 1,000,000⌉.
4. **Step functions.** `pipeline/render/*.ts`, plain functions over `{ db, media, renderer, env, clock }`:
   - `renderInput(episode, format) → RenderManifestV2 | null`:
     - It reads the current stored plan and rebuilds the brief (`briefInput` + `weekBrief`, so `leavesDevice` runs again).
     - Plan moments not in the brief are dropped.
     - For each remaining media moment, the original must exist in R2 (`head` of `mediaKey(…, 'original')`), else the moment is dropped. `media.type` comes from the asset's kind (`audio` → `voice`).
     - Answers get `answerWords` from their `workersAi` derived row.
     - Narration: the kept clips of the plan version.
     - Music: none, since the catalogue is empty.
     - It presigns every `src` and returns `episodeTimeline(…)`.
   - `startRender(episode, format)`:
     - `null` input → `{ outcome: 'nothing' }`;
