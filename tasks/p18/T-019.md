# T-019 · P18 The month

Text for T-019a–c. Published 2026-09-28. This file does not change after publication. Fix lists are written in `tasks/TASKS.md` under the task.

## Preamble

**Goal of P18.** When a month ends, the person gets a card for it on their phone (VISION §4): the storylines that moved, the people they tagged most, and the best lines they said.

**Read first:**
- `CLAUDE.md`, rules 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12 and 14.
- `docs/VISION.md` §4 and §8.
- `docs/design/DESIGN.md` §3 and §8.
- `docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md` §3, §5 and §6.
- DECISIONS D2, D3, D37, D39, D42 and D44.
- `tasks/p16/T-017.md` (T-017f `EpisodeSummary`, T-017g the Episode screens).

**Decided here (DECISIONS D44).**

- **The card is made on the phone, from the phone's own data.**
  - The moments, storylines, cast and transcripts are already on the phone. Names never need to leave it for the card.
  - Counts include moments kept only on this phone, because the card is only ever shown to the person, on this phone.
  - The one thing the phone lacks is which answer the planner chose as each week's best line (the cold open). `EpisodeSummary` gains `coldOpenMomentId`.

- **Which month, and when.**
  - A month is a calendar month of local days (`YYYY-MM`). A moment belongs to the month of its own `local_day`, as everywhere else on the phone.
  - A month has a card from the 1st of the next month (the documentary's time zone), if it has at least one moment that isn't deleted.
  - The card fills in as the last week's episode arrives, since it is worked out whenever it is shown. Nothing is stored and nothing is synced for it.

- **What the card shows.**
  - **In numbers:** how many answers, clips, photos and notes, on how many days. Kinds with none are left out.
  - **Storylines:** those with a moment in the month, most moments first, at most 3. Each shows its count and whether it started or ended this month (`openedAt` or `closedAt` on a local day of the month).
  - **People:** the cast members tagged in the most of the month's moments, at most 3, with their counts. Names come from the person's own tags only (rule 7). The words never suggest the app recognised anyone.
  - **Best lines:** the cold open of each of the month's episodes whose plan came from the model, in week order, at most 5.
    - The planner picked each one as its week's best line (D39). A recap's cold open is only the week's first answer, so it doesn't count.
    - The text is the answer's transcript on this phone, cut to 200 characters at a word boundary.
    - A line whose answer or transcript isn't on this phone is left out (rule 10).
    - Each line opens the answer in the moment viewer.
  - A section with nothing in it is left out.

- **Which episodes have a best line.** An episode has a `coldOpenMomentId` when its plan versions include a `model` version and no `recap` version, and the moment is not deleted. The value is the latest `model` version's cold open. P17's edits never change the cold open, so an edited episode keeps its line.

- **Where the person finds it.**
  - For the first 7 days of a month, Today shows a link to last month's card.
  - The Episodes screen shows a row for the most recent month with a card, above the episodes.
  - The Month screen lists up to 12 earlier months with cards.
  - There is no push. A card is a quiet part of the week, not a notification.

- **What moves to P23.** The free tier's monthly 60-second video (VISION §6) moves to P23, with the tiers. Until there are tiers, every documentary gets weekly episodes, so a monthly video would only repeat them. The free tier's video also needs a weekly run that only understands and never plans, plus originals the free tier never uploads, and both depend on the tier. Music waits for the owner's licence either way.

- **"Sharing."** The earlier texts' out-of-scope lines pointing sharing at P18 mean sharing with crew (P19) and exporting (P21). P18 has no sharing.

## T-019a · The month card rules · needs T-017f

**Goal.** Pure functions say which months have a card and what each card shows.

**Do.**

1. **Contracts.** `EpisodeSummary` and `DeviceEpisode` gain `coldOpenMomentId?: Uuid`. Existing rows still parse.
2. **`packages/story/src/month/`:**
   - `cardMonth(now, timeZone)` → the month whose card is new: the previous month when today's local day is the 1st to the 7th, else `null`.
   - `finishedMonth(month, now, timeZone)` → whether today's local day is in a later month.
   - `monthCard(input)` → the card, or `null` for a month with no live moment.
     - Input: the month (`YYYY-MM`), the documentary's time zone, its moments of that month (by `local_day`), its storylines and cast members, each answer's transcript by moment id, and its episode summaries whose week overlaps the month.
     - It follows the "What the card shows" rules.
     - The output holds the storyline titles and people's names from the live rows passed in, the counts, the flags and the line texts, so the screen only shows values.
     - The started and ended flags use `localDay(openedAt or closedAt, timeZone)`.
     - A best line belongs to the month of its answer: the cold-open moment must be one of the input's live answers of that month, so a week that spans two months puts its line on one card only.
     - Deleted moments, deleted storylines and deleted cast members are left out.
     - Ties go to the earlier `openedAt` for storylines, and to the name then the id for people.
   - `cutLine(transcript, 200)` → the transcript cut at a word boundary, with "…" when it was cut.
3. **Words** (`packages/story/src/words/month.ts`, exported as `monthWords`):
   - `title(month)` → "September 2026" (using `monthAndYear`).
   - `numbers(counts)` → "12 answers, 4 clips and 9 photos on 14 days". It uses singulars for 1 ("1 answer", "on 1 day"), leaves out kinds with none, and joins with commas and a final "and".
   - Headings: "Storylines", "People you tagged most" and "Best lines".
   - `started` "Started this month"; `ended` "Ended this month".
   - `lineFrom(weekStart)` → "From the week of 7 September".
   - `todayLink(month)` → "See your September" and `episodesRow(month)` → "Your September", with the month's name only.
   - `earlier` → "Earlier months"; `none` → "Nothing from this month."
   - Counts use the existing `moments(n)` word ("1 moment", "6 moments").

**Tests** (story fixtures):
- **`cardMonth`:** it gives August on 1 September and on 7 September, and `null` on 8 September. In Pacific/Kiritimati and America/St_Johns it takes the documentary's own local day.
- **`monthCard`:**
  - numbers by kind and days, with kinds that have none left out;
  - storylines ranked by count with the tie rule, at most 3, with the started and ended flags;
  - people ranked by count with the tie rule, at most 3, and a deleted cast member left out;
  - best lines only from episodes with `coldOpenMomentId`, in week order, at most 5, and a line without a transcript left out;
  - an episode whose week spans two months shows its line only on the card for its answer's month;
  - a storyline opened at 23:30 local on a month's last day, in a zone behind UTC, is flagged as started in that month;
  - a month whose moments are all deleted gives `null`.
- **`cutLine`:** it cuts at a word boundary with "…", and leaves a short line whole.
- **Words:** the words test covers `monthWords`, with samples for every function.

**Allowed test changes.** The words test gains `monthWords` samples.

**Acceptance.** Contracts and story typecheck, tests, lint, format, boundaries and the words test pass. `packages/story` imports only `@life/contracts` and `zod`.

## T-019b · Best lines reach the phone · needs T-017f, T-019a

**Goal.** Each episode summary says which answer was the week's best line, and the phone keeps it.

**Do.**

1. **The server.** `summaries` sets `coldOpenMomentId` for an episode whose plan versions include a `model` version and no `recap` version. The value is the cold open of its latest `model` version, and only while that moment is not deleted. Otherwise the field is absent.
   - The value can change when a later plan version is stored (a recap after a failed render). That change is already a plan change, which writes a change-log row.
2. **The phone.**
   - The next device migration adds `episodes.cold_open_moment_id`, with its migration test.
   - The device episodes repository stores and reads it, and `syncNow` lands it with the rest of the summary.

**Tests.**
- **API:**
  - A model episode's pulled summary carries its cold open id.
  - A recap episode's doesn't.
  - A model episode that fell back to a recap doesn't.
  - One whose cold-open moment was deleted doesn't.
- **Mobile:**
  - The migration keeps earlier rows.
  - A pulled summary with `coldOpenMomentId` lands with it, and a newer one without it clears it.

**Acceptance.** Contracts, API and mobile typecheck, tests, lint, format and boundaries pass.

## T-019c · The Month screen · needs T-017g, T-019b

**Goal.** The person finds last month's card on Today and the Episodes screen, and reads it on its own screen.

**Do.**

1. **`application/month.ts`:**
   - `monthCardFor(documentaryId, month)` reads the month's moments (`listByDays` over the month), the live storylines and cast, the transcripts of the month's answers, and the episode summaries whose week overlaps the month, then calls `monthCard`.
   - `monthsWithCards(documentaryId, now)` gives the finished months that have a live moment, newest first, at most 13.
2. **`app/month/[month].tsx`** with `features/month/MonthScreen.tsx`:
   - the title from `monthWords.title`, the numbers line, and then the three sections in order, each only when it has rows;
   - a best-line row opens the moment viewer for its answer;
   - "Earlier months" links to the other months with cards (up to 12);
   - a month without a card (a link to an old month whose moments were deleted) shows `monthWords.none` and a way back.
3. **Entry points.**
   - Today shows `todayLink` while `cardMonth` gives a month with a card.
   - The Episodes screen shows `episodesRow` for the newest month with a card, above the episodes.
4. **Accessibility.** Headings have the header role. Best-line rows and earlier-month links are buttons: a best line's label is the line and its week. Storyline and people rows are plain text, and each label reads the name and the count. Reduced motion applies to the screen's transitions.

**Tests** (rendered UI with fakes):
- **The card:** a month with moments of each kind, two storylines (one started this month), three tagged people and two best lines shows every section with its words. A month with only notes shows the numbers and nothing else.
- **Navigation:**
  - A best-line row opens the moment viewer for its answer.
  - An earlier-month link opens that month.
- **Entry points:**
  - Today shows the link on the 3rd of a month and not on the 8th.
  - The Episodes screen shows the row for the newest month with a card.
- **Rule 7:** no word on the screen suggests a face or voice was recognised. The words test covers the new lines.
- **Accessibility:** roles and labels are covered.

**Acceptance.** Mobile typecheck, tests, lint, format and boundaries pass. UNVERIFIED until a real device: the card's layout on a small phone and with large text.
